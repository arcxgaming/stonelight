Stonelight privacy policy

Stonelight does not collect, store or share personal information. This page says what that means in practice.

Effective 22 September 2026. Applies to Stonelight for iOS, published by ArcX Gaming.

What the game does not do

Stonelight has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising and no advertising identifiers. It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, does not track you across apps or websites, does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and does not talk to any server of ours. There is no server of ours.

What stays on your device

Your progress — the level you are on, a level you paused part-way, the light you have earned and your best on each level, your purchase and your settings — is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. It is included in your device backups if you have them turned on, and it is deleted when you delete the app. We never see it.

Purchases

The Full Climb is sold as an in-app purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and holds the receipt; the game only asks Apple whether the purchase has been made. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of that data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Stonelight is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Stonelight ever collects any data — for instance an opt-in crash report — this policy will say so before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support.arcxgaming@gmail.com.
